Problem Statement:

Despite the enduring cultural relevance of poetry and spoken word, contemporary writers and listeners face a fractured digital landscape that fails to serve verse-specific needs.

General-purpose social media and text platforms rely on rigid algorithms that neglect mood-driven discovery and strip away essential typographic nuances such as intentional line breaks, stanza formatting and visual rhythm. Furthermore, written poems and spoken-word performances are forced apart across disjointed blogging sites and mainstream audio/video services, diluting the connection between the spoken voice and the written word. This leaves poets without a dedicated stage to showcase multimedia work and build community, while isolating audiences from a focused, emotionally resonant reading and listening experience.
